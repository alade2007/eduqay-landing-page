how it works

Everything your school needs to process result

Teachers enter scores

Each subject teacher logs in and sees only their assigned classes and subjects. CA scores and exam scores are entered per student with input validation before submission.

The System calculates and computes grades

Once all scores are entered, the system calculates grades per subject. Class positions for each student based on the school's grading policy.

Class teachers review and add remarks

The class teacher reviews the scores and grades for each student in their class, add remarks and comments for each student before approving the results for publication.

Admins approves and publishes results

The administrator gives final sign-off, then repor cards are generated on the school letterhead and made available for download and printing.
